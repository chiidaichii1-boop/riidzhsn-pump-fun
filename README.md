Entry for the make.fun bounty [Pump.fun Bot Sprint: Make Bot And Win](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-pump-fun-bot-sprint-make-bot-and-win-cb203fed).

A deterministic momentum bot for pump.fun bonding curves: it buys young,
liquid mints that already show momentum (price +15% above first sight, at
least 7 of the last 10 trades buys) only after a pullback to at most 90% of
their best price since first sight, sizes ~0.09 SOL per trade, and exits on
take-profit +60%, stop-loss -30%, trailing stop -20%, or a 7,200-slot age
limit. All price comparisons are exact BigInt cross-multiplications — no
floating point, no clock, no randomness — so the two harness runs produce
byte-identical action logs.
